# <div align="center">Robot Hardware</div>

__Parts List__

* Traxxas Chassos with steering servo and sensored brushless DC motor
* Raspberry Pi 5
* LD06 LiDAR
* OAK-D Lite Camera
* VESC
* 2 DC-DC Converters
* Anti-spark switch with power switch
* 4-cell LiPo battery
* Battery voltage checker/alarm
* Micro SD Card
* Adapter/reader for Micro SD Card
* XT60, XT30, MR60 connectors

*Additional Parts used for Final Project*

* 35kg servo
* Raspberry Pi Pico 2W
* Seeed Studio XIAO nRF52840 Sense
* Raspberry Pi AI Hat+ (not used)
